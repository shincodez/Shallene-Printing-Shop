03 — About

About Shallene's Printing Shop

Shallene's Printing Shop is a printing and document shop in North Poblacion, Larena, Siquijor — right in front of Gate 1 of Siquijor State College.

Besides printing and photocopying, we laminate, scan, and bind documents, type up and edit files, and rent out computers by the hour.
