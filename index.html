About Us

About Shallene's Printing Shop

Shallene's Printing Shop is a printing and document services shop in North Poblacion, Larena, Siquijor, in front of Siquijor State College (Gate 1). We offer printing, photocopying, lamination, document editing and encoding, research services, binding, and computer rental.

We also have school and office supplies, beverages, and snacks. We're open Sunday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and closed on Saturdays.
