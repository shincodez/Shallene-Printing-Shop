## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial, high-trust identity for a neighborhood printing and document atelier serving academic, legal, and creative communities. The aesthetic bridges the tactile heritage of physical presswork with the frictionless efficiency of modern digital ordering.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Authoritative & Scholarly:** Respects the gravity of theses, business portfolios, and legal documents.
- **Approachable Craftsmanship:** Warm, neighborhood-rooted, and supportive for everyday student or commercial needs.
- **Crisp & Methodical:** Conveys exact registration, color fidelity, and structural tidiness.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic is **Modern Minimalist Editorial**. It relies on generous negative space, crisp hairline dividers, deep ink-inspired contrast, and deliberate, micro-metered bursts of process-ink color (CMYK echoes). Surfaces remain luminous and clinical, letting paper finishes, binding options, and typographic scales lead the visual hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an uncompromised white-to-slate architecture, punctuated by calibrated nods to process printing inks (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Key/Charcoal). Accents must be applied with surgical restraint—never in broad background washes, but as micro-indicators, interactive states, paper stock tags, and editorial flags.

### Base Canvas & Neutrals
- **Canvas Default:** `#FFFFFF` provides an ultra-clean, unblemished page backdrop.
- **Surface Muted:** `#F8FAFC` for alternate sheet sections and soft containment modules.
- **Surface Elevated:** `#F1F5F9` for interactive wells, drag-and-drop zones, and table headers.
- **Key Slate / Text:** `#0F172A` delivers ink-like clarity for headlines, long-form copy, and icon work.
- **Hairline Rule / Border:** `#E2E8F0` defines razor-sharp architectural layout lines.

### CMYK-Inspired Process Accents
- **Primary (Cyan Print):** `#0284C7` serves as the primary interactive driver: action buttons, text links, selected states, and upload progress bars.
- **Secondary (Process Magenta):** `#E11D48` signals urgency, express rush-order options, specialty finishes (foil, lamination), and error notifications.
- **Tertiary (Warm Yellow):** `#EAB308` handles operational tags (turnaround times, featured services, caution/proof alerts).

## Typography

The typography pairings contrast traditional editorial authority with functional screen legibility.

### Headline Hierarchy
- **Playfair Display** anchors top-tier titles, service headers, and hero value propositions. Its transitional serifs and delicate hairlines evoke curated publishing houses, broadsheet mastheads, and precision craft.
- Use medium to semi-bold weights (`500`, `600`) to maintain ink density without feathering or collapsing on standard display resolutions.

### Body & Structural Type
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a warm, modern, and highly legible geometric structure for reading, forms, tables, pricing matrices, and technical print specifications (GSM, dimensions, binding types).
- Letter spacing is intentionally neutral in body text and widened in uppercase `label-sm` metadata tags to mimic architectural registry marks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors print production grids: disciplined, column-anchored, and breathing with ample margin whitespace.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, maximum content container of `1280px`, with `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 2rem` (32px), `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` (20px), `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px).

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm follows multiples of 8px, utilizing `space-xl` between major editorial chapters and `space-md` for grouped specifications within service cards.
- Content never crowds borders; cards and interactive zones utilize generous interior padding (`space-lg`) to reflect archival paper mounts.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic paper and print aesthetic, visual separation relies on **tonal shifts and hairline rules** rather than synthetic, blurry drop shadows.

### Surface Stratification
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` for primary viewing and editorial columns.
- **Level 1 (Sheet Cards & Inset Panels):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline borders. An ultra-subtle, ambient grounding shadow is permitted for elevated interactive elements: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Modals):** Resting cards shift up on hover by -2px, transitioning their shadow to a soft press-depth: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Modals and drawers leverage an unobtrusive neutral wash backdrop with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

### Ghost Borders & Division
- Horizontal dividers mirror editorial scoring rules: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Active or focused items exchange the hairline divider for a crisp, 1.5px cyan registration ring (`#0284C7`), avoiding glow effects in favor of razor precision.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (Level 1)** curvature. Elements possess a gentle `0.25rem` (4px) baseline radius, transitioning up to `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-lg`) for major sheet cards, document upload drops, and modal dialogs. 

### Rationale
- Completely sharp corners feel overly clinical or harsh for a local service business.
- Pill-shaped or overly rounded components conflict with the disciplined, rectangular nature of paper stocks (A4, Letter, Tabloid) and print machinery.
- 4px to 8px radii simulate the natural, soft-touch bevel of trimmed stationery and die-cut business cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Button:** `#0284C7` background, pure `#FFFFFF` typography (`title-md`), 4px border radius, 0.75rem vertical × 1.5rem horizontal padding. Subtle hover shifts to `#0369A1` with no motion wobble.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive / Rush Button:** `#E11D48` background with `#FFFFFF` text, reserved exclusively for express checkout, urgent queueing, or irreversible file deletions.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height fixed at 24px with compact 4px roundedness.
- **Cyan Spec Chip (Standard Services):** `#F0F9FF` background, `#0369A1` text, `label-sm`.
- **Magenta Rush Chip (Express/Foil):** `#FFF1F2` background, `#BE123C` text, uppercase tracking.
- **Yellow Status Chip (In-Progress/Turnaround):** `#FEFCE8` background, `#A16207` text, with a 6px solid `#EAB308` status pip.

### Cards & Service Folios
- **Print Service Card:** Pure white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` frame, `rounded-lg` corners, and internal padding of `space-lg`. 
- **Header:** Features category title in `headline-sm` with a light slate rule separating the specs table.
- **Footer Section:** Crisp pricing breakdown in `title-lg` with an inline CTA aligned to the right.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border radius 4px, height 44px for high touch accessibility.
- **Focus State:** 1px border recolored to `#0284C7` with a non-blurring `0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)` offset.
- **Labels:** Displayed outside the field in `title-md` (`#0F172A`) with optional helper text in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom-drawn 18×18px squares and circles with a `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` border.
- **Checked State:** Solid `#0284C7` fill featuring a sharp white checkmark or center pip. Never use rounded fluid toggles for document options where precision binary selection is critical.

### Specialized Component: File Upload & Document Specifier
- **Dropzone:** Bordered with a 1.5px dashed `#CBD5E1` line over a `#F8FAFC` background. 
- Features document format icons (PDF, DOCX, AI), a Cyan progress bar indicator for multi-page uploads, and an instant sheet counter calculating real-time job costs.
